What we do

Weekly meetings

The club meets once a week during term: it is an informal hour of for our weekly problem sets, discussion, whiteboard problems, and whatever anyone is currently reading.
Drop in for any of it.

Faculty talks

At a few points each semester we host a faculty member or a guest for a talk on their work, pitched so that anyone in the room can follow it. You can find the schedule on Talks.

Everyone is welcome, at every level.

Drop in to any meeting. We post what's happening each week on Instagram; for the Discord invite, ask any of the officers.
