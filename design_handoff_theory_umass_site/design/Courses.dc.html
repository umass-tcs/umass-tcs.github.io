Courses

Theory courses at UMass we recommend, and what you need before taking them.

Courses at UMass

  • CS250 Introduction to Computation

    This course teaches you about common proof techniques and basics of computation

    Prereqs CS160 and MATH132

  • CS311 Introduction to Algorithms

    Algorithms are the basis of solving computational problems

    Prereqs CS250

  • CS501 Formal Language Theory

    It teaches you the foundations of computation and thinking about fundamentally hard problems.

    Prereqs CS311 OR A in CS250 and dual enrollment with CS311

  • CS513/613 Logic in Computer Science

    It introduces different logics, logic programming, descriptive complexity, and other topics in logic.

    Prereqs CS311

  • CS514 Algorithms for Data Science

    It goes over techniques applicable to processing large amounts of data and computing the problems relevant to such settings.

    Prereqs CS240 and CS311

  • CS575 Graph Theory and Combinatorics

    For discrete structures and computations, graphs provide important abstractions, while combinatorics provide many fundamental techniques

    Prereqs CS250

  • CS601 Complexity Theory

    A well structured course about thinking about resource bounded computation.

    Prereqs CS501

  • CS611 Advanced Algorithm

    This course teaches advanced techniques and paradigms in algorithm design

    Prereqs CS311

  • CS614 Randomized Algorithms

    Randomization can sometimes help speedup our computation. This class teaches such problems and techniques.

    Prereqs CS514 OR CS611
